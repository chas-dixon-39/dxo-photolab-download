## What is DxO PhotoLab?

DxO PhotoLab is a RAW photo editor built around DxO's optical-science background, combining exclusive lens-correction profiles with local adjustment tools such as the U Point technology and DeepPRIME noise reduction. It automatically corrects common optical defects — distortion, vignetting, chromatic aberration and lens softness — without manual intervention, using device-specific calibration data measured in DxO's own labs.

## Key Features

### 🖼️ Image Quality & RAW Processing
<img src="https://pub-27e14545e036427f828cd9cf06f1395f.r2.dev/web/dxo.com/uploads/2025/09/Photo_Lion_Apres_Denoising_954x636px_CloseUp.webp" alt="DeepPRIME noise reduction result" width="480">

Denoising is driven by machine-learning models — DeepPRIME 3 and DeepPRIME XD3, the latter covering both Bayer and X-Trans sensors — aimed at removing noise while keeping fine detail and accurate color, alongside a faster classic "Standard" mode and a live 100% preview magnifier.

### 🎨 Color & Tonal Adjustment
<img src="https://pub-27e14545e036427f828cd9cf06f1395f.r2.dev/web/dxo.com/uploads/2025/09/Photo_Dark_Apres_954x636px.webp" alt="Tonal adjustment example" width="480">

A ColorWheel tool lets you manipulate individual color channels, alongside exposure, tone curve, contrast/microcontrast, white balance, saturation and a dedicated black-and-white channel mixer. Smart Lighting and ClearView Plus automate shadow/highlight balancing and haze removal.

### 🤖 AI Masking & Local Adjustments
<img src="https://pub-27e14545e036427f828cd9cf06f1395f.r2.dev/web/dxo.com/uploads/2025/09/after-montagne-noir.webp" alt="AI Masks local adjustment example" width="480">

Machine-learning-based selections let you click, draw, or pick predefined subject types — sky, face, hair — for fast, precise local edits. Classic U Point Control Points/Lines remain available for layer-free intelligent masking.

### 🎞️ Creative Editing & 🗂️ Management
LUTs, presets, Style/Toning, an advanced clone/repair tool, crop/perspective tools, plus a catalog-free PhotoLibrary with keywording, star ratings and Projects.

### 📤 Export & Workflow
Custom export presets, parallel exports, ICC/sRGB/Adobe RGB profiles, watermarking, Virtual Copies, Quick Compare, and a fully customizable multi-monitor workspace.
